About Chaniz Workers

Chaniz Workers Production & Supply Co. Ltd. was founded with one clear mission: to connect real people with real job opportunities across Tanzania.

Many hardworking individuals struggle to access reliable employment—not because they lack skills, but because opportunities are scattered, informal, or unreachable. At the same time, employers face challenges finding trustworthy, job-ready candidates.

Chaniz Workers was created to close this gap.

We provide a structured, transparent, and accessible platform where job seekers and employers meet with confidence. From hospitality and services to production and skilled labor, we help businesses hire efficiently while supporting individuals in building stable careers.

Beyond job listings, we guide job seekers through the hiring journey—supporting CV preparation, job matching, and employer connections—while offering employers a simple and reliable recruitment process.

Rooted in Tanzania and built for local realities, Chaniz Workers is more than a job board. We are a workforce partner—empowering people, strengthening businesses, and building futures.
